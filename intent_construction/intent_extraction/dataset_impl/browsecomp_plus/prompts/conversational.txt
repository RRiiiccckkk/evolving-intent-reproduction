You are given a goal and conditions from a complex research query. Transform them into a conversational multi-turn format where conditions are revealed progressively.

Output JSON format:
{
  "initial_query": "conversational version of the goal (what to find)",
  "hints": [
    {"hint": "condition 1 as a complete sentence"},
    {"hint": "condition 2 as a complete sentence"},
    ...
  ]
}

Rules:
1. The initial_query should be based on the goal - state WHAT to find
   - Keep it concise and natural, like how a user would ask a research question
2. Each hint corresponds to a condition, rephrased as a complete sentence
   - Start with a capital letter
   - End with a period (.) or appropriate punctuation
   - Be grammatically correct when read alone
3. PRESERVE all specific details exactly (names, dates, numbers, places, organizations)
4. Order hints logically (general context → specific constraints → distinguishing details)
5. Together, initial_query + all hints should contain ALL information from the original query

Example:

Q: Could you provide the name of the individual who: - As of December 2023, the individual was the coordinator of a research group founded in 2009. - Co-edited a book published in 2018 by Routledge. - Served as the convenor of a panel before 2020.

Goal: What is the name of the individual?

Conditions:
[
  {"argument": "As of December 2023, the individual was the coordinator of a research group founded in 2009."},
  {"argument": "The individual co-edited a book published in 2018 by Routledge."},
  {"argument": "The individual served as the convenor of a panel before 2020."}
]

Output:
{
  "initial_query": "What is the name of the individual?",
  "hints": [
    {"hint": "As of December 2023, the individual was the coordinator of a research group founded in 2009."},
    {"hint": "The individual co-edited a book published in 2018 by Routledge."},
    {"hint": "The individual served as the convenor of a panel before 2020."}
  ]
}
