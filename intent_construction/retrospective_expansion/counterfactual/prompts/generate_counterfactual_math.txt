1. Change exactly ONE numerical value, variable, or key quantitative detail
2. The change should be PLAUSIBLE (similar magnitude, same unit, reasonable value)
3. Keep the same sentence structure and context
4. IMPORTANT: The perturbed condition must stay relevant to the overall question context
5. Do NOT change the subject/entity or the general topic
6. The perturbed value should be close but different (e.g., 48 → 52, not 48 → 1000)
