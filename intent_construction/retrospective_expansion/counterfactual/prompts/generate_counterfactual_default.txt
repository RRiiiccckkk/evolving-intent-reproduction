1. Change exactly ONE detail: a number, a name, a date, or a specific attribute
2. The change should be PLAUSIBLE and CONTEXTUALLY APPROPRIATE

4. CRITICAL: Do NOT change the main subject/entity to something unrelated
5. The perturbed value should be similar but different (small, meaningful change)
